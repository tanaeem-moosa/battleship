The Mathematical Parity Advantage: Because the smallest ship in Hasbro Battleship is the Destroyer (length 2), any placement on a 2D grid must cross at least one cell where (row + col) % 2 == 0. By restricting hunt shots to only the 50 parity cells instead of all 100, Checkerboard cuts the required search space by 50% while guaranteeing no ship can ever slip past undetected! This yields an average efficiency improvement of ~10 to 12 turns per game, translating into a decisive ~65% to 75% head-to-head win rate.
